🧭 New to this? What's "YAML frontmatter" and why does it matter
Most of these skills store their metadata (name, description, when to use it) in a small header block at the top of the file, written in YAML — think of it like the label on a filing folder. The model reads just the header first to decide whether a skill is relevant. Only once it's actually invoked does the full body (the real instructions) get loaded. That's "progressive loading" — it keeps the model's context window from being clogged with instructions for skills it isn't using.
